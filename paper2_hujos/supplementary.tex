% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S7
\section*{S7. Two implementation details of the robust intersection}

Two implementation details of $V_{\mathrm{rob}}$ matter, and getting either
wrong silently breaks Proposition~3 of the main text. A world that produces
\emph{no} violation must contribute the \emph{empty set} to the intersection;
skipping non-firing worlds computes $\bigcap$ over firing worlds only, which is
an existential quantifier wearing the clothes of a universal one. And
$\mathcal{W}$ must span the full range in which a claim can be legitimate: with
$\mathcal{W}=\{0,0.25\}$ the largest admissible depth anywhere in $\Theta$ is
$22.5$\,mm, so every claim above that is ``guilty in all worlds'' and the
intersection becomes vacuous --- it fires on $102$ of $150$ worlds while $48$
innocent worlds exist. Extending $\mathcal{W}$ to $\{0,0.25,0.5,0.75,0.9\}$
restores the intended semantics.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S8
\section*{S8. Relation to prior monitoring work (full text)}

AWD monitoring has been approached from three directions. \emph{Instrumented}: a
low-cost wireless water-level sensor for lowland rice under
AWD~\cite{delacruz2022}, which solves the measurement problem but multiplies
device cost per plot. \emph{Remote sensing}: identification of AWD adoption in
the Vietnamese Mekong Delta~\cite{lovell2019}, water-status monitoring with
ALOS-2 scattering-power decomposition~\cite{islam2026alose}, and region-scale
machine-learning mapping of water-saving practices~\cite{mlrice2025}; these cover
large areas cheaply but revisit every two to four days and cannot resolve the
$72$\,h dry phase that determines credit eligibility. \emph{Programme level}: the
one-million-hectare high-quality low-emission rice programme~\cite{qd1490}, which
had brought over $350\,000$ hectares under cultivation by 2026~\cite{mae2026progress},
and MRV identified as the missing link for low-emission rice in Southeast
Asia~\cite{irri2026mrv}.

On the agronomic side, meta-analysis quantifies the water and yield effects of
AWD~\cite{carrijo2017}, while adoption economics~\cite{lampayan2015} and the
technical and institutional barriers to scaling~\cite{enriquez2021} explain why
instrumented monitoring has not spread. None of these lines evaluates the
\emph{reliability of the action record itself}, which is the question asked here.

The gateway supplying the device-anchored timestamp is the controller of the
companion work~\cite{twingate2026}, whose command dispatch runs over a
burst-noise channel of the Gilbert--Elliott type~\cite{gilbert1960,elliott1963}.
Our own prior scheduling results for safety-critical agricultural IoT networks
address the same transmission regime~\cite{rabs,cawvou}, but neither establishes
an evidence record, which is the function relied on here. Because that controller
already signs every command it issues, the anchor adds storage and transmission
of a record that exists regardless --- which is what keeps the marginal cost of
attestation near zero.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S9
\section*{S9. Extended discussion}

\subsection*{S9.1 Why publishing the threshold is not the problem}

One might conclude that $\varepsilon$ should be kept secret. It should not, for
two reasons. A verifier must be able to reproduce a decision, which requires
published parameters. And secrecy would not help: by Proposition~1 of the main
text the time-shifting attack preserves the total volume and every individual
depth, so it does not need to know $\varepsilon$ in order to stay beneath the
infiltration bound --- it needs only to avoid moving water, which is visible to
any farmer. The vulnerability is structural, not a consequence of disclosure.

\subsection*{S9.2 Relation to remote sensing (full text)}

Satellite radar is the obvious low-cost alternative, so rather than argue about it
we measured it. Table~5 of the main text scores every route by one quantity: how
many of the $150$ credit-eligible dry phases actually present across the $60$
simulated fields it could confirm. A phase counts as confirmable only if at least
one observation falls inside it, because a $72$\,h phase lying wholly between two
passes cannot be evidenced at all --- which costs the farmer a credit even when
the practice was performed correctly. Single-satellite Sentinel-1, revisiting every
twelve days~\cite{clauss2017s1}, confirms $90/150$ ($60\%$); the full A+B
constellation at six days~\cite{hashemi2022s1} reaches $124/150$ ($83\%$), and
still misses one phase in six at zero device cost. A continuous water-level tube
confirms all $150$, at one device per plot~\cite{delacruz2022}. The signed command
record also confirms all $150$ --- because the phases are defined by the intervals
between actuations, which the controller already timestamps --- at no additional
device, since the controller is the one that opened the pump.
Two cautions belong with that comparison. The confirmation column measures the
ability to \emph{earn} a credit, not to detect fraud: the tube and the command
record tie at $100\%$ here and differ entirely in cost and in what they can prove
about a doctored log. And photograph-plus-self-report scores $0\%$ not because it
is worthless but because it produces no observation series to reconcile against;
every confirmation under it must come from a field visit.
Radar therefore keeps the role VM0051 Appendix 4 assigns it: a per-plot cross-check
that lowers the frequency of field verification~\cite{vm0051}.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S10
\section*{S10. Harness defects and design details}

\subsection*{S10.1 Per-event, not per-hour, recording behaviour}

Rounding and delay are applied per \emph{event}, not per hour: applying a
$5$\,mm rounding to a $40$\,mm event spread over eight hours rounds each
$5$\,mm fragment to zero and silently deletes the record, an artefact that
produced empty logs in an early version of this harness.

\subsection*{S10.2 Two harness defects that had to be fixed}

Two harness defects had to be fixed before the reported numbers meant anything,
and both are recorded in the source because they are easy to reintroduce.
First, a fraud generator that finds no improving move returns the \emph{original}
honest log, so an unconditional count of ``attacks caught'' silently includes
cases where no attack occurred; every adversarial row therefore carries a
\texttt{changed} flag and all attack statistics are conditioned on it. Second,
comparisons must use the baseline of the variant itself: the paper-condition
honest log is generated by a separate call, and scoring it against the
field-condition record of a different soil realisation falsely ``caught'' $56$
of $60$ honest logs.

\subsection*{S10.3 Volume is a diagnostic, not a target}

A configuration that maximises claimed volume is not automatically profitable:
an early adversary that inflated claims by $344$\,mm drove $n_{\mathrm{dry}}$ to
zero and would have earned nothing, so volume is reported only as a diagnostic.

\subsection*{S10.4 The self-consistency artefact of the first version}

The first version of the audited layer generated ``real'' trajectories with the
default \texttt{SoilProfile}, noiseless ERA5 forcing, irrigation as a one-hour
impulse of exactly $\{20,40\}$\,mm, a threshold of exactly $0.80$, and a farmer
who records the right depth at the right hour every time. Its $0/60$ false
accusations were therefore measurements of self-consistency. In the present
design the field is generated by a separate process that differs from the twin
along the seven axes listed in Table~S6.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S11
\section*{S11. Proofs of Propositions 1 and 2}

\subsection*{Proof of Proposition 1 (exact blindness of aggregate defences)}

$U(L)=\sum_i u_i$ is a symmetric function of the multiset, so it is invariant
under any reassignment of depths to hours; $M$ is a property of the field, not
of the log; hence $G=M-U$ is unchanged. Likewise the infiltration bound (Eq.~13
of the main text) is evaluated per event and depends only on $(u_i,\tau_i)$,
never on $t_i$ or on $\hat w$, so the count of violations is a function of the
multiset alone.

\subsection*{Proof of Proposition 2 (credit gain and evasion are the same action)}

(i) is definitional: the set $\mathcal{A}$ requires no claim inside the
interval, so removing one extends or creates a qualifying phase. For (ii),
delaying a claim to $t+\delta$ means the bucket continues to lose water for
$\delta$ further hours with no inflow, so $\hat w_{t+\delta}^{-}\le\hat w_t^{-}$
and $c_{t+\delta}\ge c_t$; the bound on the deficit growth follows because the
loss per hour is at least the mean $\overline{\Delta}$ until field capacity is
reached, after which $c$ saturates at $FC-\hat w$. Since the storage-excess
filter fires only when $u_i>c_{t_i}+\varepsilon$ and $u_i$ is unchanged, a
larger $c_{t_i}$ can only reduce the violation set.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S12
\section*{S12. Proof of Proposition 4 (completeness of the anchor)}

The first clause is the definition of the anchor verdict: any disagreement of
the hour sets or of a depth at a shared hour triggers \text{forge}. For the
second clause, total volume, individual depths and timestamps are all functions
of the hour set and the per-hour depths, so agreement on those two fixes all
three, and no forgery of the three quantities has occurred.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S13
\section*{S13. The wilting-point filter: full derivation}

The published layer included a third physical filter, flagging depletion below
the wilting point as physically impossible. It was doubly inoperative. Its
threshold was set at $1.02$ while the reported indicator
$\hat d_t=\min(1,\max(0,\cdot))$ is clipped to $[0,1]$, so the condition was
unsatisfiable by construction: $0$ firings in $281$ logs. We repaired it by
evaluating the unclipped depletion, and then measured whether the repaired filter
can activate at all. Across three stations, two climate years, five textures and
five initial states --- $150$ configurations --- with an empty log, i.e.\ the
driest possible forcing, the unclipped depletion peaks at $0.9987$
(Table~S3). A dry-season delta rice field does not reach the
wilting point. The filter is therefore a \emph{safety} device that guarantees no
absurd state is accepted; it is not evidence of detection, and the layer must be
described as having two operative physical filters and one inert one.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S14
\section*{S14. The two corrections to the admissible-depth bound}

First, Eq.~(16) of the main text uses $\max$, not a difference: an initially
dry field ($w_0=0.90$) admits \emph{larger} claims than a wet one, and the
previously published expression $(d^{*}-w_0)\,\mathrm{span}+\varepsilon$
yields $43.5$\,mm for $\mathrm{span}=70$\,mm and $w_0=0.25$, which
corresponds to no physical state at all, since the operative deficit at that
hour is either $0.25\,\mathrm{span}$ (initially) or $d^{*}\mathrm{span}$
(once the field has dried). Second, the bound is \emph{per event}, not per
season: the bucket drains continuously through deep percolation and
evapotranspiration, so a season-long claim total is not bounded by $u_{\max}$
and an adversary issuing many sub-threshold claims can inflate the total
without bound.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S15
\section*{S15. Why the paired baseline is the honest one}

Forged variants are only generated where a trajectory carries enough commands
to manipulate (three or more), and those trajectories are already drier than
average: their honest baseline carries $4.87$ creditable dry phases against
$3.23$ for all honest logs. An unpaired comparison against the broader baseline
therefore attributes the pre-existing dryness to the attack and overstates the
inflation roughly twofold. Every figure in Table~4 of the main text is computed
on the paired subset (same station, year, seed and soil), conditioned on the
\texttt{changed} flag.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S16
\section*{S16. The nine log types}

Three are the naive forgeries of the prior work: added events on rainy days,
one third of entries inflated to the whole storage span, and a $96$\,h window
of claims deleted. Four are adversarial and, unlike the naive ones, are
constructed by an optimiser rather than a fixed recipe: consolidation onto a
$24$\,h and a $96$\,h grid; a rational adversary that selects whichever
consolidation window maximises $n_{\mathrm{dry}}$ subject to preserving the
total and respecting the infiltration bound; and the time-shifting hill-climb,
which searches integer offsets in $[-36,36]$ and accepts a move only if the
total is preserved bit-for-bit, every depth stays below the infiltration bound,
and $n_{\mathrm{dry}}$ strictly increases. The remaining two honest classes
(paper-condition and field-condition) are the baselines.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S17
\section*{S17. The cost of robustness, and agreement across uncertainty sets}

\subsection*{S17.1 The measured detection loss}

Replacing the point model by the intersection over $\Theta$ is not free, and
the loss must be reported. On the nine omission-fraud logs that were actually
altered, the point model catches $9/9$ while the robust rule catches $4/9$
($44\%$); under the full $\Theta$ the figures are $4/4$ and $2/4$ ($50\%$).
The same trade-off runs in the same direction as the proportional threshold,
and for the same reason: a defence that refuses to accuse on the strength of
one model configuration also refuses some true positives. Naive forgeries
remain at $100\%$ under both. We regard this as the correct side of the trade,
since a false accusation falls on a farmer with no means of appeal while a
missed forgery falls on a verification body that samples anyway, but it is a
loss of detection power and not a Pareto improvement.

\subsection*{S17.2 Reduced and full uncertainty sets agree}

Every conclusion holds under both the reduced $\Theta$ ($24$ replays per log,
$281$ logs, $10$ seeds) and the full $\Theta$ ($150$ replays, $85$ logs,
$3$ seeds): false accusation $0/60$ and $0/18$, anchor completeness $116/116$
and $37/37$, robust-tier time-shift escape $8/9$ and $3/3$, paired credit
inflation $+33\%$ ($t=5.29$) and $+38\%$ ($t=3.46$). The reduced set was used
for the large runs because it contains the extremes of every axis, which is
where an innocent world is most likely to exist; the full set confirms that no
interior configuration changes a verdict.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S18
\section*{S18. Why the anchor does not re-introduce false accusation (full text)}

Proposition~4 of the main text is strong enough to look dangerous: any
discrepancy is treated as forgery, including the forgetting and rounding that
the main text showed to be ubiquitous. The resolution is that the anchor does
not compare a log against a \emph{model}; it compares a transcript against the
\emph{source}. An honest farmer who forgets an entry is not accused of fraud,
because the system of record is the gateway, not the farmer's memory: the claim
is simply completed from $\mathcal{G}$. In operational terms the anchor turns
the log from \emph{evidence the farmer must get right} into \emph{a view of a
record the device already holds}. False accusation then requires the gateway to
disagree with itself, which is a device-integrity question rather than a
behavioural one, and is why the guarantee holds at $0/60$ while the behavioural
tiers hold at $26.7\%$ and $5.0\%$.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S19
\section*{S19. What the attack steals}

The attack does not steal water. Total pumped volume is unchanged, so the
farmer pays the same and the atmosphere sees the same methane. What is stolen
is \emph{history}: the credit is issued for a dry-phase pattern that did not
occur. Under VM0051 the crediting unit is precisely that pattern, so the loss
is real even though no physical resource moved.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S20
\section*{S20. Experimental design: full detail}

\subsection*{S20.1 Removing the circularity, axis by axis}

The field is generated by a separate process that differs from the twin along
seven axes, each with a published source (Table~S6): soil texture drawn from
the five FAO-56 Table 2 classes present in the delta; $K_c$ staged per Table 12
rather than fixed at $1.05$; irrigation efficiency $\eta\in[0.60,0.85]$, so
the farmer reports water \emph{pumped} while only $\eta u$ reaches the root
zone; field rainfall drawn as $0.7$--$1.4$ times the grid cell plus Gaussian
noise; bund seepage $0$--$0.02$\,mm\,h$^{-1}$; recording behaviour with $5\%$
forgetting, rounding to $5$\,mm, a $35\%$ chance of a one-day delay and
$15\%$ depth error within $\pm35\%$; and an initial depletion $w_0$ drawn
from $[0,0.45]$ rather than assumed to be field capacity. Rounding and delay
are applied per \emph{event}, not per hour (S10.1).

\subsection*{S20.2 What survives of the original claim}

The premise that motivated this work was attractive: an irrigation command is
already a digital event, so MRV evidence should be nearly free. That premise
survives, but not in the form we first published. It survives because the
controller that issues the command can also sign it, and the signed record is
what makes the evidence admissible --- not because a hydrological model can
detect a lie. Where the original draft claimed that physics attests the log,
the correct statement is that physics \emph{constrains} the log: it rules out
depths no soil can hold and states no field can reach, which is worth having,
and it is silent about the one transformation that pays. Keeping $\varepsilon$
secret would not close the hole either: the attack does not need to know the
threshold, and a verifier must be able to reproduce a decision in any case
(S9.1).

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- S21
\section*{S21. Limitations (full text)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Simulated farmers.} Forgeries are injected into simulated reference
trajectories. A field pilot is needed to measure behavioural response, in
particular whether farmers shift their \emph{actual} pumping once the anchor is
known to exist --- an attack our model cannot represent, since it changes $M$
rather than $L$.
\item \emph{The anchor assumes an honest gateway.}
Proposition~4 of the main text is completeness against log forgery, not against
a compromised controller. Device integrity, key custody and replay protection
are assumed from the companion work and are not re-established here.
\item \emph{Not applicable to manual irrigation.} Where a sluice is opened by
hand there is no command record, so the anchor is unavailable. This excludes a
substantial share of delta farms and bounds the addressable population of the
scheme.
\item \emph{The attack is demonstrated in the water-scarce year only.} In the
water-surplus year (2025) farmers need so little irrigation that most simulated
logs fall below the three-command minimum at which a timing attack is even
constructible: of $37$ genuinely altered logs under the full $\Theta$, all $37$
come from 2024. The escarpment between the two years is itself a finding --- MRV
fraud risk is concentrated where water is scarce --- but it also means the
attack results are not yet evidence about a wet season. Under the reduced
$\Theta$ twelve altered logs do come from 2025, and they behave the same way,
which is suggestive rather than conclusive.
\item \emph{$\Theta$ is a judgement.} Five textures, two $K_c$ regimes, three
efficiencies and five initial states bracket the delta but do not exhaust it. A
field outside $\Theta$ loses the guarantee of Proposition~3 of the main text,
which is exactly the situation the proportional threshold and the query verdict
exist to absorb.
\item \emph{Device prices are planning estimates, not quotations.} The tier
ordering and the break-even inequality are unaffected; the absolute margin is.
\item \emph{No emission quantification.} The layer produces activity data.
Coupling it to a methane model and locally calibrated emission factors is
separate work, as is the cost table's assumption that credits scale with
$n_{\mathrm{dry}}$.
\end{enumerate}
