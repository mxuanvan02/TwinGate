\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:design}

The design is a comparison of four defence layers against nine log types, on a
field that deliberately violates every assumption of the twin.

\subsection{Removing the circularity}

The prior evaluation generated ``real'' trajectories with the default
\texttt{SoilProfile}, noiseless ERA5 forcing, irrigation as a one-hour impulse of
exactly $\{20,40\}$\,mm, a threshold of exactly $0.80$, and a farmer who records
the right depth at the right hour. Its $0/60$ false accusations were therefore
measurements of self-consistency. Here the field is generated by a separate
process that differs from the twin along seven axes, each with a published source
(Table~\ref{tab:setup}): soil texture drawn from the five FAO-56 Table 2
classes present in the delta; $K_c$ staged per Table 12 rather than fixed at
$1.05$; irrigation efficiency $\eta\in[0.60,0.85]$, so the farmer reports water
\emph{pumped} while only $\eta u$ reaches the root zone; field rainfall drawn as
$0.7$--$1.4$ times the grid cell plus Gaussian noise; bund seepage
$0$--$0.02$\,mm\,h$^{-1}$; recording behaviour with $5\%$ forgetting, rounding to
$5$\,mm, a $35\%$ chance of a one-day delay and $15\%$ depth error within
$\pm35\%$; and an initial depletion $w_0$ drawn from $[0,0.45]$ rather than
assumed to be field capacity. Rounding and delay are applied per \emph{event},
not per hour: applying a $5$\,mm rounding to a $40$\,mm event spread over eight
hours rounds each $5$\,mm fragment to zero and silently deletes the record, an
artefact that produced empty logs in an early version of this harness.

Weather is hourly ERA5 from the Open-Meteo archive for Can Tho, Soc Trang and Ca
Mau over 2024 (water-scarce) and 2025 (water-surplus), no missing values, window
1 March to 29 May ($2\,160$\,h), the dry season in which AWD is practised.

\subsection{Four defence layers}

Layer~1 is the published point-model detector: replay under the default profile,
absolute pump-gap threshold $\tau_0=5$\,mm. Layer~2 is the robust detector of
\eqref{eq:robust} with the proportional threshold of \eqref{eq:tau}, run both on
a reduced $\Theta$ ($12$ configurations $\times2$ initial states $=24$ replays
per log, used for the $10$-seed runs) and on the full $\Theta$ ($30\times5=150$
replays, used as a cross-check). Layer~3 is the device anchor of \eqref{eq:anchor}.
Layer~4, for reference, is physics alone with no volume measurement.

\subsection{Nine log types, four of them adversarial}

Three are the naive forgeries of the prior work: added events on rainy days,
one third of entries inflated to the whole storage span, and a $96$\,h window of
claims deleted. Four are adversarial and, unlike the naive ones, are constructed
by an optimiser rather than a fixed recipe: consolidation onto a $24$\,h and a
$96$\,h grid; a rational adversary that selects whichever consolidation window
maximises $n_{\mathrm{dry}}$ subject to preserving the total and respecting
\eqref{eq:v5}; and the time-shifting hill-climb of Sec.~\ref{sec:timeshift},
which searches integer offsets in $[-36,36]$ and accepts a move only if the total
is preserved bit-for-bit, every depth stays below the infiltration bound, and
$n_{\mathrm{dry}}$ strictly increases.

Two harness defects had to be fixed before these numbers meant anything, and both
are recorded in the source because they are easy to reintroduce. First, a fraud
generator that finds no improving move returns the \emph{original} honest log, so
an unconditional count of ``attacks caught'' silently includes cases where no
attack occurred; every adversarial row therefore carries a \texttt{changed} flag
and all attack statistics are conditioned on it. Second, comparisons must use the
baseline of the variant itself: the paper-condition honest log is generated by a
separate call, and scoring it against the field-condition record of a different
soil realisation falsely ``caught'' $56$ of $60$ honest logs.

\subsection{Metrics}

For each layer we report the escape rate, i.e.\ the fraction of forged logs
neither tier flags, and the false-accusation rate on honest logs. For credit
impact we report $n_{\mathrm{dry}}$ of \eqref{eq:ndry}, the number of
credit-eligible dry phases, against the honest field baseline. A configuration
that maximises claimed volume is not automatically profitable: an early adversary
that inflated claims by $344$\,mm drove $n_{\mathrm{dry}}$ to zero and would have
earned nothing, so volume is reported only as a diagnostic.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{The published detector is unsafe, not merely optimistic}

Table~\ref{tab:falseacc} gives the central negative result. On logs generated
under paper conditions the point-model detector accuses nobody, reproducing the
prior $0/60$. On the same detector applied to field-condition logs it accuses
$26.7\%$ of honest farmers at tier~1. The cause is \eqref{eq:gap} with an
absolute threshold: forgetting one entry, rounding, or recording a day late all
produce $G>5$\,mm without any wrongdoing, and the measured gap on honest logs
reached $75$\,mm. The proportional threshold of \eqref{eq:tau} reduces this to
$5.0\%$, and the device anchor to $0/60$.

\subsection{Physics cannot see the attack that pays}

Table~\ref{tab:credit} is the second negative result, and it is the one that
matters economically. Relative to an honest field baseline of $3.23$ creditable
dry phases per season, time-shifting raises the count to $5.48$, an inflation of
$69\%$; under the full uncertainty set the figure is $6.14$ against $3.44$, or
$78\%$. Consolidation reaches $+52\%$ and $+53\%$, and the rational adversary
$+63\%$ and $+70\%$. Table~\ref{tab:defence} shows the corresponding escape
rates: time-shifting escapes both the point model and the robust physics layer
in $91\%$ of cases, and the rational adversary in $100\%$, exactly as
Proposition~\ref{prop:coincide} predicts. The infiltration filter $V_5$ fires
$648$ times across the evaluation but exclusively on the crude
depth-inflation forgery, never on a time-preserving-volume attack --- it is
useful, and orthogonal to the threat that pays.

\subsection{The anchor closes every class}

Table~\ref{tab:defence} also gives the constructive half. Of $281$ audited logs,
$116$ were genuinely altered by an attack; the device anchor caught $116/116$ and
accused none of $60$ honest field-condition logs. Under the full $\Theta$ the
figures are $37/37$ and $0/18$. Naive forgeries are caught by every layer; the
distinction is entirely in the adversarial rows, where physics and volume both
fail and only the anchor holds.

\subsection{The cost of robustness is measurable}

Replacing the point model by the intersection over $\Theta$ is not free, and the
loss must be reported. On omission fraud the point model catches $78.3\%$ of
altered logs while the robust rule catches $40\%$ --- the same trade-off direction
as the proportional threshold, and for the same reason: a defence that refuses to
accuse on the strength of one model configuration also refuses some true
positives. Naive forgeries remain at $100\%$ under both. We regard this as the
correct side of the trade, since a false accusation falls on a farmer with no
means of appeal while a missed forgery falls on a verification body that samples
anyway, but it is a loss of detection power and not a Pareto improvement.

\subsection{Reduced and full uncertainty sets agree}

Every conclusion above holds under both the reduced $\Theta$ ($24$ replays per
log, $281$ logs, $10$ seeds) and the full $\Theta$ ($150$ replays, $85$ logs,
$3$ seeds): false accusation $0/60$ and $0/18$, anchor completeness $116/116$ and
$37/37$, time-shift escape $91\%$ and $100\%$, credit inflation $+69\%$ and
$+78\%$. The reduced set was used for the large runs because it contains the
extremes of every axis, which is where an innocent world is most likely to exist;
the full set confirms that no interior configuration changes a verdict.
