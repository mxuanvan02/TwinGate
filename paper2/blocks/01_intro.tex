\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Viet Nam has committed to one million hectares of low-emission rice by 2030, and
the agronomy is settled: raising the severity of intermittent drying cut methane
emission by $41$--$71\%$ against continuous flooding with no grain-yield
penalty~\cite{balaine2019}, and water regime dominates seasonal methane flux
across Asian rice fields~\cite{yan2005}. What is not settled is measurement. Under Decision 4801/QD-BNNMT, documentary evidence for a
credit claim is ranked, and the record of the irrigation and drainage actions
actually taken sits at the top of that ranking (spot-check Form~3.3, Annex~2:
actual irrigation and drainage records above plans, schedules, field notes and
signed statements)~\cite{qd4801}, and under Verra's VM0051 the same record must survive
third-party verification~\cite{vm0051}. Producing it costs money a smallholder
cooperative does not have: monitoring alone is about $77\%$ of MRV
expenditure~\cite{jem2026mrv}, and the current procedure relies on periodic
photographs of a water-level tube.

The obvious repair is to notice that a modern irrigation command is already a
digital event: if a gateway actuates the pump it can also record the actuation,
and that record is a Tier-1 activity datum in the sense of Decision 4801 at no
marginal cost --- the premise of the companion work~\cite{twingate2026}, which
uses an FAO-56 twin as a safety gate deciding whether a command may be
transmitted over a lossy channel. We took the next step and asked whether the
same twin could \emph{audit} what a farmer claims after the fact, with no
sensor deployed: a log of claimed depths is replayed through the water balance
under public ERA5 forcing, and a claim the soil could not have absorbed is
evidence of fabrication. A first version of this layer reported no false
accusation on $60$ honest logs and detection of $60/60$ forged ones --- correct
and worthless, because the trajectories labelled ``real'' were generated by the
same model that then audited them: the detector was grading its own homework
(Supplementary~S10.4).

This paper reports what happens when that circularity is removed: we rebuild
the field so that it violates every assumption of the twin, and replace the
naive fraud patterns with an adversary that has read the detector. Three
findings follow, and they are also the contributions. First, the point-model
detector is not merely optimistic, it is \emph{unsafe}: it falsely accuses
$26.7\%$ of honest farmers, because an absolute pump-gap threshold treats a
farmer who forgets one entry exactly like one who irrigated secretly; the
robust rule that fixes this --- an intersection over an uncertainty set
$\Theta$ of $30$ soil, crop-coefficient and efficiency configurations
(Sec.~\ref{sec:robust}) --- pays for it in detection power, which we measure
rather than hide. Second, physics-based attestation has a hole no amount of
modelling closes: an adversary who preserves the total volume and every
individual depth can \emph{shift commands in time}, inflating the
credit-eligible dry phases that VM0051 and Decision 4801 pay for by $+33\%$
in paired comparison ($t=5.29$; $+38\%$, $t=3.46$, full uncertainty set). No
physical tier escapes --- the point model catches four of nine altered logs but
is the very detector that accuses $26.7\%$ of honest farmers, while the robust
tier that cuts false accusation to $5.0\%$ lets eight of nine through --- and
we prove this is unavoidable for any detector that is a function of the
replayed trajectory (Sec.~\ref{sec:threat}, Sec.~\ref{sec:timeshift}).
Third, the repair is cheap and not physical: matching the log against the
gateway's signed, time-stamped command record catches $116/116$ genuinely
forged logs and accuses none of $60$ honest ones, at no cost beyond the gateway
the companion work deploys (Sec.~\ref{sec:anchor}). The paper also corrects
three published numbers: the admissible depth is a range $[49.0,86.0]$\,mm,
not $61$\,mm; the third physical filter cannot activate on dry-season delta
rice; and the absolute pump-gap threshold is inadmissible
(Sec.~\ref{sec:umax}, Sec.~\ref{sec:v2reach}).
